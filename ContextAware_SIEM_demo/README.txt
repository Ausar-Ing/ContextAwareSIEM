Context-Aware Alert Prioritization: offline demo

How to open
1. Extract (unzip) the whole folder first. A page opened from inside the zip
   cannot reach the other pages.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. No internet connection is needed.

What is here
  index.html                     the demo on the russellmitchell replay
  harrison.html                  the same demo on the harrison replay
                                 (or click "harrison" at the top of index.html)
  Math_Sheet.html                the formulas behind the scores

Keys in the demo
  left / right arrow   previous / next step
  1 to 5               jump to a scene
  S                    switch between the two replays
  T                    light / dark theme
