\section{Risk-based alerting comparator B$_\mathrm{RBA}$ (Q9)}
Ported from Uetz et al.\ (arXiv:2609.02465) and their CATS code (commit \texttt{628cf48}): five modules scored per alert, weighted arithmetic mean, groups take the maximum. Two changes for comparability: windows look only into the past, and the normalizers CATS estimates from the whole file are estimated from the pre-attack period. Aperiodicity is computed on at most 3{,}000 evenly spaced alerts per rule type, others taking the nearest preceding value (cost control). Selection on the tuning set: (1) the best window and shape per module on its own; (2) all 1001 module weightings in steps of 0.1.

\section{How to reproduce}
\label{sec:repro}
{\raggedright\small
From the repository root, with the data files of \texttt{provenance/ait/README.md} (Section~1) and \texttt{tools/requirements-ait.txt} installed; \texttt{D=data/ait\_ads; C=\$D/alerts\_csv; T=tools; P=provenance/ait}. The steps that read caches run in the directory holding them (the evaluation harness writes \texttt{evalcache\_*}; \texttt{ait\_param\_lab.py} writes \texttt{plabrows\_*}/\texttt{plabfit\_*}).
\begin{enumerate}
\item Caches and forests: \texttt{python3 \$T/ait\_param\_lab.py \$T \$P/host\_roles.json pl.json --seeds 5 --variants delta86400} (after one run of the harness below so that \texttt{evalcache\_*} exist).
\item Weight search: \texttt{python3 \$T/ait\_weight\_lab.py \$T \$P/host\_roles.json weight\_lab.json --seeds 5} $\rightarrow$ compare with \texttt{\$P/weight\_lab\_verdict.txt}.
\item Comparator: \texttt{python3 \$T/ait\_rba\_tune.py \$T rba\_config.json rba\_tune.json} $\rightarrow$ compare with \texttt{\$T/rba\_config.json} and \texttt{\$P/rba\_tune\_report.txt}.
\item All systems: \texttt{python3 \$T/ait\_eval.py \$D/ait\_ads.zip \$D/labels.csv \$C \$T \$P/host\_roles.json ev.json --scenarios harrison russellmitchell wheeler shaw --seeds 5}, then \texttt{python3 \$T/ait\_report.py \$T ev.json} and \texttt{python3 \$T/ait\_holm.py \$T ev.json} $\rightarrow$ compare with \texttt{\$P/eval\_tuning\_set\_report.txt} and \texttt{\_holm.txt}.
\item Variety experiment: \texttt{python3 \$T/ait\_weight\_lab.py \$T \$P/host\_roles.json wv.json --seeds 5 --variety variety\_1d\_triangle}.
\item Workstation: \texttt{python3 \$T/ait\_thresholds.py \$T \$P/host\_roles.json ws\_thresholds.json --seeds 5}, then \texttt{python3 \$T/ws\_eval.py \$T data/workstation/baseline\_frozen\_20261017T2315.json ws\_thresholds.json ws.json --seeds 5} $\rightarrow$ compare with \texttt{provenance/workstation/ws\_eval.json}.
\item No-reconnaissance condition: step 4 with \texttt{CONTEXTSIEM\_DROP\_LABELS=service\_scan,dirb,wpscan} $\rightarrow$ compare with \texttt{\$P/eval\_tuning\_set\_noscans\_report.txt}.
\end{enumerate}
With Python 3.11, numpy 2.4, scikit-learn 1.8 and scipy 1.17 the seeded numbers should reproduce exactly; other library versions may differ in the last digits. Runtimes on two cores: steps 2 and 5 about a minute each, step 3 about ten minutes, steps 4 and 7 about fifteen minutes each, step 6 about twenty.\par}
